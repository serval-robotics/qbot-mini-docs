# Overview

What QBot Mini is, who it is for, and what makes it different.

!!! note "Being written"

    This page is an outline. The sections below are what it will cover.


## What it is

The machine in one paragraph: a twelve-joint quadruped with a closed five-bar leg, ROS 2 on top, and a simulation that runs the same control stack.

## Who it is for

Robotics and AI labs, teaching programmes, and integrators who need a legged platform they can modify below the application layer.

## What makes it different

Open at the control seam, simulation parity with the hardware, ROS 2 native, and a supply chain that clears government procurement rules.

## What it is not

Not a certified industrial product, not a turnkey inspection robot, and not a toy. Being explicit here is what makes the rest credible.

## Where to go next

Integrators start with the ROS 2 interface; teaching programmes start with the course modules.
